# Pepperoncino Pizza (Pizza al Peperoncino)

A spicy, vibrant Italian-style pizza featuring fiery peperoncino, aromatic tomato sauce, creamy melted mozzarella, and fresh basil. Whether using fiery Calabrian chilis in oil, fresh red chilis, or zesty pickled pepperoncini, this recipe brings heat, acidity, and rich savoriness in perfect harmony.

---

## Yield & Timing

- **Yield**: Two 12-inch (30 cm) pizzas
- **Prep Time**: 30 minutes (plus 24–48 hours cold fermentation)
- **Cook Time**: 2–8 minutes per pizza (depending on oven setup)

---

## Ingredients

### 1. Pizza Dough (65% Hydration)
- **Tipo 00 flour** (or high-protein unbleached bread flour): 500g (~4 cups)
- **Water** (lukewarm, ~25°C / 77°F): 325g (1 1/3 cups)
- **Fine sea salt**: 12g (2 tsp)
- **Instant dry yeast**: 1.5g (1/2 tsp)
- **Extra virgin olive oil** (recommended for standard home ovens): 10g (2 tsp)

### 2. Sauce
- **Whole peeled San Marzano tomatoes**: 400g (1 can), crushed by hand or pulsed lightly
- **Fine sea salt**: 3g (1/2 tsp)
- **Extra virgin olive oil**: 15ml (1 tbsp)
- **Fresh basil leaves**: 3–4 leaves, torn

### 3. Cheese & Toppings
- **Fresh mozzarella** (fior di latte or whole-milk mozzarella, drained well and torn): 250g
- **Parmigiano-Reggiano or Pecorino Romano** (freshly grated): 40g (~1/2 cup)
- **Peperoncino**:
  - *Option A (Calabrian fiery style)*: 2–3 tbsp crushed Calabrian chilis in oil or fresh red chilis, thinly sliced
  - *Option B (Pickled Tuscan pepperoncini style)*: 1/2 cup pickled pepperoncini, stemmed, seeded, and sliced into rings
- **Garlic**: 2 cloves, very thinly shaved (optional)
- **Finishing touches**:
  - Fresh basil leaves
  - Dried wild oregano: a pinch
  - Calabrian chili oil (olio di peperoncino) or good EVOO: for drizzling
  - Hot honey (optional, for sweet-heat contrast)

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. **Mix**: In a large bowl, dissolve yeast in lukewarm water. Gradually incorporate the flour until a shaggy dough forms. Cover and let rest for 20 minutes (autolyse).
2. **Knead**: Sprinkle salt (and olive oil, if using) across the dough. Knead on a lightly floured surface for 8–10 minutes until smooth, supple, and elastic.
3. **Cold Fermentation**: Form into a ball, place in an oiled bowl, cover tightly, and allow to ferment at room temperature for 2 hours, then refrigerate for 24–48 hours for deep flavor development and light, airy crust.
4. **Ball the Dough**: About 2–3 hours before baking, divide into two equal balls (~420g each). Place in a proofing box or covered container at room temperature to relax and expand.

### Step 2: Prepare Sauce & Toppings
1. **Crush Tomatoes**: Hand-crush the whole peeled San Marzano tomatoes, straining excess liquid. Stir in fine sea salt, extra virgin olive oil, and torn basil. Keep uncooked to preserve fresh flavor.
2. **Drain Mozzarella**: Tear fresh mozzarella into bite-sized strips and set on paper towels for 30 minutes to absorb surface moisture.
3. **Prep Peperoncino**: Slice peppers into rings. If using pickled pepperoncini, pat them dry with paper towels to prevent soggy dough.

### Step 3: Oven Preparation
- **High-Heat Pizza Oven (450°C–480°C / 840°F–900°F)**: Preheat baking stone for 30–40 minutes.
- **Home Kitchen Oven (250°C–280°C / 500°F–550°F)**: Place a pizza stone or baking steel on the top rack. Preheat for at least 45–60 minutes at maximum bake setting, switching to high broil 10 minutes prior to baking.

### Step 4: Stretch, Top & Bake
1. **Stretch**: Dust your work surface and peel with fine semolina or coarse flour. Gently press a dough ball from center outward, leaving a 1-inch puffed rim (cornicione). Stretch out to a 12-inch circle.
2. **Sauce**: Ladle ~80g of tomato sauce onto the center and spiral outward toward the rim.
3. **Cheese & Peperoncino**:
   - Scatter grated Parmigiano-Reggiano evenly over the sauce.
   - Distribute drained mozzarella pieces.
   - Scatter prepared peperoncino slices and shaved garlic evenly across the top.
4. **Bake**:
   - **Pizza Oven**: Slide pizza onto stone and bake for 60–90 seconds, rotating frequently for an even char.
   - **Home Oven**: Bake on preheated steel/stone for 5–7 minutes until crust is blistered and cheese is bubbly and caramelized in spots.

### Step 5: Finish & Serve
1. Transfer to a cutting board.
2. Garnish immediately with fresh basil leaves, a light pinch of dried oregano, and a drizzle of spicy peperoncino oil.
3. Slice and serve hot!
